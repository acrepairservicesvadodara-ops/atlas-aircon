## The Issue
An educational institute in Fatehgunj with 21 split and cassette units faced high electricity bills and inadequate cooling in peak summer classes.

## Diagnostic
Multiple units had severely choked condenser fins, reduced heat transfer, and slight refrigerant pressure drops due to neglected maintenance over 18 months.

## Engineering Fix
1. Deployed an 8-member commercial squad over a weekend.
2. Executed 120-Bar pressure wash descaling across all 21 outdoor condensing units.
3. Repaired 3 leaking flare joints and topped up refrigerant to manufacturer baseline.
4. Cleaned and sanitized all indoor filters and cooling coils.

## Result
Total plant cooling capacity restored to 100%, with customer reporting a noticeable 18% reduction in monthly power consumption.
