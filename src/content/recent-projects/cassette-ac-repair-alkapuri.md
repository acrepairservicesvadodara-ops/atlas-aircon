## The Issue
A boutique showroom in Alkapuri experienced water dripping directly onto customer display counters from a 3-ton ceiling cassette unit.

## Diagnostic
Inspection revealed a choked internal condensation drain basin and calcium sludge blocking the internal lift pump, causing the safety float switch to trigger shut-down.

## Engineering Fix
1. Installed containment drop sheets to protect the showroom floor and merchandise.
2. Cleared the drain piping using pressure flushing and serviced the internal condensate pump.
3. Conducted a 120-Bar alkaline chemical deep cleaning of the evaporator coil to restore airflow.

## Result
Airflow velocity increased by 40% and all water leakage stopped completely.
