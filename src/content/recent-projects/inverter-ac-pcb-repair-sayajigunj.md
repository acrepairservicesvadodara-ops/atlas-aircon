## The Issue
A customer in Sayajigunj reported an inverter split AC displaying an error code with no cooling. The authorized company technician had quoted ₹9,000 for a complete outdoor PCB replacement.

## Diagnostic
Our technician arrived within 60 minutes and tested the circuit board using an oscilloscope and digital multimeter. We discovered a blown IPM (Intelligent Power Module) and a faulty bridge rectifier caused by a recent voltage spike.

## Engineering Fix
1. Removed the outdoor control board and replaced the burnt IPM chip on-site using micro-soldering equipment.
2. Performed a 45-minute deep vacuum on the refrigerant lines.
3. Charged virgin R32 refrigerant by exact weight according to manufacturer specifications.

## Result
The unit resumed full cooling efficiency, achieving a 14°C vent temperature within 10 minutes. The client saved over 65% compared to purchasing a new board.
