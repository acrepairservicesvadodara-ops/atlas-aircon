## What is the Daikin U4 Error Code?

The `U4` error code on a Daikin inverter AC indicates a **communication fault** between the indoor unit (IDU) and the outdoor unit (ODU). 

Because Inverter ACs rely on constant digital communication to adjust compressor speed, a break in this signal causes the entire system to shut down for safety.

### Common Causes:
1. **Broken or loose communication wire** (usually the 3rd or 4th wire connecting the units).
2. **Burnt Communication IC** on either the indoor or outdoor PCB (usually caused by voltage spikes).
3. **Power supply issues** to the outdoor unit.
4. **Mismatched units** (e.g., pairing a 1.5-ton indoor unit with a 2-ton outdoor unit).

## How Atlas Aircon Fixes U4 Errors

Unlike company mechanics who will blindly tell you to replace the entire ₹8,000+ outdoor PCB, our engineers perform **component-level micro-soldering**.

1. We use a digital multimeter to test the DC voltage pulse on the communication wire (it should fluctuate between 15V and 30V DC).
2. If the wire is fine, we dismantle the PCB and locate the blown optocoupler or communication IC.
3. We desolder the damaged component and replace it with a high-quality OEM chip.

> **Warning:** Do not attempt to reset the AC repeatedly if a U4 error appears. Continuous power cycling can cause secondary damage to the IPM (Intelligent Power Module). Call an expert immediately.
