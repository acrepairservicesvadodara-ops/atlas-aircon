## Primary Root Causes Behind `E1–P4`

1. **Component Drift or Circuit Fault:** Full diagnostic table for MarQ by Flipkart Split & Convertible Inverter ACs (E1–E6, EC, F0–F5, P0–P4). Thermal cycling, moisture condensation inside the indoor wiring trough, or high-voltage spikes on MGVCL/GIDC feeders degrade sensitive SMD electronics and NTC thermistors.
2. **Airflow & Heat Rejection Stress:** Blocked indoor return filters, matted blower wheels, or dust-impacted outdoor condenser fins push operating pressures and electronics temperatures past factory safety thresholds.
3. **Wiring, Flare & Connector Oxidation:** Loose spade lugs, unshielded wire splices between indoor and outdoor units, carbonized terminal blocks, or micro-leaks at brass flare nuts frequently trigger intermittent `E1–P4` lockouts during peak afternoon load.
4. **Power Supply Instability:** Weak run capacitors, failing stabilizer relays, or degraded SMPS filter capacitors cause DC bus ripple and microcontroller communication drops.

## Safe Homeowner / Facility Checks Before Calling an Engineer

1. **Perform One Clean Hard Reset:** Switch off the dedicated AC MCB breaker for **5 full minutes** to discharge the high-voltage DC bus capacitors on the inverter board, then restore power once. *Do not power-cycle repeatedly if `E1–P4` returns.*
2. **Inspect Air Filters & Outdoor Clearance:** Wash clogged indoor nylon mesh filters and confirm nothing (pigeons, plastic sheets, exhaust heat) is obstructing the outdoor condensing unit fan.
3. **Check Stabilizer Voltage Readout:** Ensure input/output voltage remains between **205V and 245V AC**. Severe low voltage under afternoon load forces protection trips.
